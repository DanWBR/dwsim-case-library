# Verification

**English** | [Português](VERIFICATION.pt-BR.md)

A case can be **community** or **verified**.

## Community

Published as submitted. Complete and passing the confidentiality checklist, but not independently reproduced. Most cases start here.

## Verified

Reproduced by a maintainer or a second contributor. To promote a case to verified, someone other than the original author confirms:

1. The attached flowsheet opens in the stated DWSIM version.
2. It solves without errors.
3. The key results in the write-up match what the flowsheet produces, within a reasonable tolerance.

When those hold, a maintainer changes the case's **Status** to `verified`, adds the badge, and notes who verified it and with which DWSIM version.

## Help verify

You do not need to be a maintainer. Pick a community case, open its flowsheet, run it, and report what you found on the case's issue or pull request:

- DWSIM version you used.
- Whether it opened and solved.
- Whether the results matched, with numbers.

Good verification reports are as valuable as new cases.

## Badges

- `verified` - reproduced by a second person.
- `community` - published as submitted.

Verified cases are listed first in each category index.
