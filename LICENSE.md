# Licensing

This library holds two kinds of material, licensed differently.

## Case write-ups (text and figures)

The written cases and their figures are licensed under the
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
You may share and adapt them, including commercially, as long as you give appropriate credit.

## DWSIM flowsheet files

Flowsheet files (`.dwxmz`) attached to a case are shared by their contributors so others can open,
run, and learn from them. Unless a case states otherwise, treat them as CC BY 4.0 as well: reuse is
fine with attribution to the contributor.

## What contributors confirm

By submitting to this library, you confirm that:

- You have the right to publish the process description, data, figures, and files you include.
- Any comparison against a commercial simulator is permitted by that simulator's license.
- No confidential or proprietary data is exposed.
- You agree to license your written contribution under CC BY 4.0.

## Not covered

DWSIM itself is a separate project with its own license. This library only covers the contributed
cases and files described above.
